Roomate Support

Help with the Roomate app for shared households

Contact us

For questions, bug reports or account problems, email support.roomateapp@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 2–5 business days. Telling us your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened helps us help you faster.

Common questions

  • How do I add a roommate? Open Settings, find your household, and share its invite code. Your roommate chooses "Join with code" and enters it.
  • How are balances worked out? Each expense is split between the people you choose, evenly or by custom amounts. Roomate keeps a running total of who owes whom.
  • How do I settle up? Pay your roommate the way you normally do, then record the settlement in Roomate so both balances update. You can attach a photo of the payment.
  • I didn't get my verification code. Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then request a new code.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. Households you own must be handed over or deleted first. Deleting your account is permanent. You can also email us to request deletion.
